<strong>Research Vision & Purpose</strong><br>
My research is driven by the goal of bridging the gap between complex deep learning architectures and highly practical, real-world applications. Specifically, I focus on developing parameter-efficient models—such as lightweight adapters and few-shot segmentation frameworks—that maintain state-of-the-art accuracy even when annotated data is scarce. 

By pushing the boundaries of <strong>Medical Image Analysis</strong>, <strong>Core Computer Vision</strong>, and <strong>Document Recognition</strong>, my work aims to create robust AI tools that can be reliably deployed in clinical diagnostics and complex visual environments. Below is a categorized selection of my peer-reviewed contributions, including several published in top-tier (Q1) international journals.
